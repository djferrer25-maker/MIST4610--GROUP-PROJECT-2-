Question: 
Question 1: "How has the physical fitness gap between male and female students in California's public schools evolved over the last two decades (1999-2019), and how does this trend differ across Grade 5, Grade 7, and Grade 9?"

Why it's interesting: It addresses a significant social and public health topic (gender disparities in health). It's a complex trend analysis, not just a snapshot, which allows you to see if public health initiatives are (or are not) closing this gap over time.

Question 2: "Is there a geographic pattern to physical fitness outcomes? Specifically, which counties show the widest disparity in fitness rates between racial/ethnic groups (e.g., 'White' vs. 'Hispanic') for 9th graders in the most recent year of data?"

Why it's interesting: This question explores potential socio-economic and geographic inequalities in public health. It's perfect for a powerful map visualization in Tableau and can highlight areas of high (or low) inequality, which has direct policy implications.
